Zoom out to see every workspace — concept v3

An interactive HTML/CSS mock, not an image-generated concept. It uses the live warm-chat tokens and the direction-C tab header. The window has no navigation bar. A zoomed-in workspace looks the same as the app does today. When you zoom out, the whole workspace shrinks as-is and sits beside the others, so the zoomed-out view is the navigator. Workspaces nest: “Work” holds two of them, and “Archive” is nested deeply enough that its workspaces show as icons until you zoom into it.

Mock controls (not app UI): scroll in overview · Ctrl+scroll or pinch inside a workspace · double-click a workspace to go in · double-click the background to go out · Alt+←/→ back/forward
New in v3: zoomed out, drag a box over any tiles to open a view of only those · in that view, drag a tile header onto another tile's edge to dock it, or into a tile's middle to float it · Esc or double-click a gap to go back

What each step does. Each scroll step moves one named level: workspace → “Work” → everything, the same “one step of the scroll wheel” as in the video. Scrolling in zooms toward whatever is under the pointer. At the top level, double-clicking the background returns you to the last workspace you were in, which matches his double-click toggle between the desktop and the overview. Back/forward goes through your zoom history (Alt+arrows, mouse side buttons). The transcript also mentions buttons and saved frames. Here a saved frame is just a named group you can zoom to, so no strip is needed.

How it maps onto ClosedAI. Each workspace is today's chat-layout split tree, unchanged. What's new is one tree of workspaces above it, using the same split and dock code. Chats are DOM, so a CSS scale shrinks them and they keep streaming while zoomed out. The browser is a native view and can't be CSS-scaled, so zoomed out it shows a capturePage snapshot and goes live again when you zoom back in. Inside a workspace, plain scrolling still scrolls the chat and double-clicking a divider still balances the split, so zooming out from there uses Ctrl+scroll, pinch or a shortcut.

Box-select and smooth docking (new in v3, from your screenshots). In the video, dragging a box over three windows in the zoomed-out view opens a view of only those, and the other windows reflow smoothly while one is being dragged. Here the box picks individual tiles (chats and browsers), not whole workspaces, because a view of three chats at full size is something you can work in. The tiles keep their left-to-right and top-to-bottom order. The opened view is an ordinary layout: in the app it would be a temporary ChatLayout built from the existing pane ids, so the chats move rather than being copied, and Back returns them. When you drag a header, the tile collapses to its tab and the others close the gap. Hovering near another tile's edge opens a frosted slot where it will land, and dropping in a tile's middle leaves it floating. The app already computes this live preview (layout-drag-preview.ts). Tiles are positioned with plain left/top/width/height and no transition, though (chat-canvas.tsx:14), so today the preview snaps instead of gliding.
