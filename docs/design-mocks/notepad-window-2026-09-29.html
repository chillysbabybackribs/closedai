Notepad window with its note chat (wireframe)

This is a behaviour wireframe drawn in HTML/CSS. Each window has one chat, and every message goes to the note in the active tab. A running task stays on the note it started on. Switching tabs while a task runs shrinks the chat to its compact form. The × hides the chat behind the round button, and clicking the button or pressing Ctrl+J brings it back.

Try this

  1. Open the chat with the round button. The chip in the composer shows the active note.
  2. On plan.md, send any request. The card shrinks to the compact form and the model edits plan.md line by line.
  3. While it runs, switch to scratch. The task stays on plan.md, and the tab keeps its working dot.
  4. Expand the card, then switch tabs again. It goes back to compact.
  5. Press ×. The button shows a progress ring and the note name. Open the chat again.
  6. When it's done, open the ⋯ menu and choose Clear chat. The notes stay as they are.
