The browser is showing a frozen picture of the page, so your clicks hit an image. Now finding what left it there.

The live page is back, but what left the frozen picture there is still unknown, so it could happen again.

The app puts a picture over the page on purpose while a menu or a dragged window sits on top. That picture should go away afterwards. Here it didn't.

Hiding and re-showing the browser removed the picture. Five possible causes were ruled out; the last one (a window drag or a workspace switch that never "finished") is the likely culprit but wasn't proven.
